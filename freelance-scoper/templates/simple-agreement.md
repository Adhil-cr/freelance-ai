# Freelance Services Agreement (plain-language draft)

**Draft for review. Not legal advice.** Have a lawyer check it once before you reuse it, especially for larger projects, health, finance or children's data. Check stamp duty and signing rules for your state.

This agreement is between **[Freelancer name, address]** ("I") and **[Client name or company, address]** ("you") and starts on **[date]**.

1. **The work.** I will build the project described in the attached Scope Sheet ("the Scope"). Anything not listed in the Scope is not included.
2. **Deliverables and acceptance.** I will deliver the items in the Scope. Each item is accepted when it meets the acceptance criteria in the Scope, or after [7] days of use without written objections.
3. **Timeline.** I aim to finish by [date]. Dates move by the same number of days when your feedback, content or payments are late.
4. **Price and payment.** The price is [amount] before GST. You pay [advance]% before I start, [midpoint]% at [milestone], and [final]% on acceptance. Invoices are due within [7] days. GST is added only if I am registered for it. Late payments pause the work.
5. **Revisions.** The price includes [2] rounds of design changes and [2] rounds of feedback on the finished build. Extra rounds are charged at [Rs X] per hour.
6. **Changes to the Scope.** Any change is written down first with its price and effect on the date. I start the change only after you approve it in writing.
7. **Your responsibilities.** You give timely feedback, content and access, and you create the domain, hosting, payment and store accounts in your own name.
8. **Ownership.** You own the final deliverables after you have paid in full. I keep ownership of my general tools and code libraries and may reuse them. Until full payment, I keep ownership of the work.
9. **Portfolio.** I may show non-confidential screenshots of the project in my portfolio, unless you tell me not to in writing.
10. **Confidentiality.** Each of us keeps the other's confidential information private, and uses it only for this project. This does not apply to information that is already public.
11. **Data.** If the product stores personal data, you decide what is collected and are responsible for notices and consent. I will process data only for this project, use reasonable security, and help with deletion and export.
12. **Warranty.** For [30] days after acceptance I fix defects in what I delivered at no charge. New features or problems caused by third-party services or your changes are not covered.
13. **Limit of responsibility.** I am not responsible for indirect losses (like lost profits). My total responsibility is limited to the amount you have paid me under this agreement. [Review with a lawyer.] If this product gives health, nutrition or fitness information, it is general information and not medical advice.
14. **Ending the agreement.** Either of us may end this with [7] days' written notice. You pay for the work done up to the end date, and the advance is not refundable once design work has started. [Review with a lawyer.]

Governing law: [state], India. Disputes: first talk, then mediation, then courts of [city].
