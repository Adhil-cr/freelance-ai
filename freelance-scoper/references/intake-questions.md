# Intake questions (ask before pricing)

Why this exists: the same brief can be a Rs 1 lakh personal tool or a Rs 5 lakh product, depending on who uses it and how far it must scale. A price given before these answers is a guess, and a confident wrong guess is worse than a question.

## How to ask
- If an interactive question tool is available (for example AskUserQuestion), ask up to 4 questions at once with the options below. Always allow a free-text answer.
- If not, ask in plain text with lettered options, in one short message. Number the questions.
- Every question has an implicit option: **"I don't know yet, add it to my client questions."** When chosen, record it as an open question, lower the confidence level, and draft the message to send to the client.
- Ask the gate questions first. Do not ask more than 5 things at a time.

## Gate questions (must be answered, or marked unknown, before any figure)

**G1. Who will use it?** (this changes the price more than anything else)
- A. Only the client, for personal use
- B. A small business serving local customers
- C. A scalable startup product (many users, may charge money or raise funding)
- D. A company's internal system (ERP, CRM, HR, inventory)
- E. Something else / not sure

**G2. Budget stance**
- A. Under Rs 50,000
- B. Rs 50,000 to 1,50,000
- C. Rs 1,50,000 to 5,00,000
- D. Above Rs 5,00,000
- E. Client has not decided and wants my suggestion

(Adapt the bands to the client's currency and market. If the client will not name a budget, offer two scope options at different prices and ask which is closer.)

**G3. Platform**
- A. Website or web app that also works on a phone
- B. Android app
- C. Android and iPhone apps
- D. Desktop software
- E. Not sure (recommend the cheapest option that meets the need)

**G4. Deadline**
- A. Within a month, firm reason
- B. 1 to 3 months
- C. Flexible

**G5. Scale** (skip for personal use)
- A. Up to 10 users, B. 10 to 500, C. 500 to 10,000, D. More, or unknown

## About the freelancer (ask once, then reuse)
- Experience level: first paid project, 1 to 3 projects, or established?
- Stack they are comfortable with, hours per day they can really give, GST-registered or not, own hourly rate if they have one.
If the user already quotes their own rates or hours, switch to expert mode (see SKILL.md).

## Follow-ups by usage type

**Personal use**
- Is the client sure they need custom software, or would an existing app do? What does the existing app not do for them?
- Phone only, or also laptop? Does it need to work offline?
- Who keeps data safe if the phone is lost (backups)?

**Small business**
- Who updates content or data after launch: the owner, staff, or you?
- Existing website, logo, branding, content, customer list?
- Payments or bookings online? Which gateway and who owns the account?

**Scalable startup product**
- How will it make money, and when must it launch (investor demo, pilot)?
- What is the first version that proves the idea, and what comes after?
- Who owns the code and brand, and will there be a team or CTO later?
- Expected users in 6 and 12 months? Any user data that is sensitive?
- Is the client open to a phased agreement (discovery, MVP, then growth)?

**Company internal system (ERP/CRM)**
- Which departments and processes, and how many staff will use it?
- What exists today (Excel, Tally, paper, another software) and must data be migrated?
- Roles and approvals: who can see or change what?
- Reports and invoices the company must produce (GST, audit)?
- Who is the decision-maker and who will test it for the company?
- Who supports it after launch?

**Fitness and nutrition products**: see `domains/fitness-nutrition.md`.

## Turning unknowns into a client message
For each unknown, write one plain question the freelancer can send. Group them in one message, numbered, with options where possible. Example:
> Hi [Name], to give you an accurate price I need three answers: 1) Will only you use this, or will other people/customers also use it? 2) Do you have a rough budget range in mind? 3) Android, iPhone, or a website that works on both? A one-line answer for each is enough.
