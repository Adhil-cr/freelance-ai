# Domain: education, schools and learning platforms (LMS)

Use for school and institution sites, coaching-centre apps, online courses and quiz platforms.

## School or institution website
Mostly content: admissions, notices, gallery, contact, results links. Price low, but ask who updates content (a CMS or admin panel), and whether a fee-payment or admission form with payment is expected, because that is a different project.

## Learning platform: what is easy to underprice
- Roles: student, teacher, admin, and sometimes parent. Each needs its own screens and permissions.
- Content delivery: video hosting and streaming cost real money per month. Do not store videos on the app server; use a video host and price it as a recurring cost.
- Quizzes and grading: question types, timers, randomisation, anti-cheating, and result reports take longer than expected.
- Progress tracking, certificates and notifications.
- Payments, coupons and course access rules (lifetime, subscription, batch).
- Live classes: integrate an existing tool rather than build one.

## Questions to ask
1. Who are the users, and how many students at the same time (exam days are peaks)?
2. Is there existing content (videos, PDFs, question banks), and who owns the rights?
3. Live classes, recorded lessons, or both? Which tool do they already use?
4. How do students pay, and what happens on refund or batch change?
5. Do you need reports for teachers or institutions?
6. Are minors using it? (see privacy below)

## Risks
Content copyright, video piracy, exam-day traffic, and children's data. Under India's data protection law, processing children's data needs verifiable parental consent when the main duties start (May 2027, verify), so plan consent screens now.

## Paperwork
Agreement with a content-ownership clause, privacy notice with parental-consent wording, and terms of use. Use the standard set in `paperwork-guide.md`.

## Calculator
Use category "Learning platform (LMS)" or "School / institution site", and the features "Course & lesson management" and "Quiz / assessment engine".
