# Domain: real estate and listing sites

Use for property listing sites, agent portals, rental listings and classifieds-style directories.

## What is easy to underprice
- Listings: fields, photos, maps, filters and search. Image upload and resizing take time and storage.
- Enquiry handling: forms, notifications to agents, and a lead list.
- Agent or owner accounts, plus moderation of fake listings.
- Maps and location search (map provider costs can be recurring).
- SEO: listing pages are the traffic source, so page speed and URLs matter.
- Payments for featured listings or subscriptions, if wanted.

## A multi-seller marketplace is bigger
If several agents post their own listings with commissions or payouts, treat it as a marketplace (see `ecommerce-booking.md`) and quote discovery first.

## Questions to ask
1. One company's listings, or many agents posting their own?
2. Who adds and updates listings, how often, and who removes expired ones?
3. Which maps and search filters matter most to buyers?
4. Do you need lead tracking or a CRM?
5. Will listings carry paid promotion?
6. Existing data or website to migrate?

## Risks
Fake or outdated listings, image storage cost, map API billing, and spam enquiries. Mitigate with moderation, expiry dates, image limits, usage caps on the map key and a captcha on forms.

## Paperwork
Standard set in `paperwork-guide.md`; add terms for listings (who owns photos, takedown rules) and a privacy notice for enquiry data.
