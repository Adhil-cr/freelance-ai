# Project stages for a freelance build

Use this as the backbone of the "Stages and payments" section. Adapt names and timing to the project size.

## Stage 0: Discovery call (unpaid, short)
- Goal: understand the real problem, budget range, deadline, who decides, and who pays.
- Output: your own notes, then a short written summary sent to the client.
- Red flags: no budget idea, "just like app X" with no detail, decision-maker not on the call, wants a fixed price for unclear scope.

## Stage 1: Proposal and agreement
- Send: scope (MVP / phase 2 / excluded), price, timeline, payment split, revision policy, what you need from the client.
- Client confirms in writing (email or WhatsApp is acceptable evidence, a signed agreement is better).
- **Advance payment is received before any prototype work starts.** It filters out clients who are not serious.

## Stage 2: Planning and design prototype
- Sitemap or screen list, then clickable prototype (Figma or a live preview).
- Show it to the client on a call, collect changes, apply the included revision rounds.
- **Gate: written design sign-off.** No backend build starts without it. This prevents "I thought it would look different" disputes mid-build.

## Stage 3: Build
- Backend, database, APIs, frontend integration, in small weekly increments.
- Send a short progress update weekly (what is done, what is next, anything needed from the client).
- Anything new the client asks for goes through the change-request rule, in writing, before work starts.

## Stage 4: Midpoint review
- Demo on a staging link. Midpoint payment due when the agreed milestone is met (for example, core features working).

## Stage 5: Testing and acceptance
- Your own testing first, then client user acceptance testing with a short checklist.
- Define acceptance in advance: what "done" means for each MVP feature.

## Stage 6: Deployment and handover
- Deploy to the client's hosting account and domain, registered in the client's name.
- Hand over: credentials, source code or repo access (per agreement), a short admin guide, and a walkthrough call.
- **Final payment due on acceptance.** Transfer IP and admin ownership on full payment.

## Stage 7: Warranty and maintenance
- Free fixes for defects in delivered work for an agreed warranty period (typically 15 to 30 days).
- After that, offer a monthly maintenance package (security updates, backups, small fixes, monitoring) or hourly support.

## Typical timing for a small project
Discovery 1 to 3 days, proposal 2 to 3 days, prototype and sign-off 1 to 2 weeks, build per the hours estimate, acceptance 1 week, warranty 2 to 4 weeks. Client delay should extend the schedule, and the agreement should say so.
