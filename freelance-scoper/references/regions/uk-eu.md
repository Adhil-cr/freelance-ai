# Clients in the UK and EU

Last reviewed: 2026-11-10. UK VAT rule checked against HMRC's manual; EU points are general knowledge. **Verify before relying on it. Not legal or tax advice.** Read `international-clients.md` first.

## Market
- Rates are high, a little below the US. The calculator uses cautious multipliers (UK 1.8, EU 1.9). Eastern European clients and agencies often pay less; adjust.
- Clients value clear contracts, data protection and polite, direct communication.

## Tax and contract points to verify
- **VAT (UK):** HMRC's rule is that business-to-business services are supplied where the customer belongs, and the UK business customer accounts for the VAT itself (reverse charge). The customer must show it is a business, usually with a VAT number or other proof; without that, treat it as a consumer sale and ask a CA what applies. Keep the proof on file.
- **VAT (EU):** the same business-to-business principle applies in most EU countries, but check the client's country and the invoice wording the client expects.
- **Data protection:** the UK and the EU have strict personal-data rules (UK GDPR / GDPR). If the app handles personal data of people there, you need a data-processing agreement and must understand where data is stored. Raise this early; it affects hosting and price.
- **Contract:** state governing law, currency (GBP or EUR), payment terms (net 14 to 30), and IP transfer on payment.
- Some clients require a statement that you are an independent contractor; keep control of your hours and tools.

## Habits
- Time zones overlap well with India's morning. Offer a fixed weekly call and written updates.
- Many EU clients prefer bank transfer (SEPA) or Wise. Check fees for both sides.
- Public holidays and August holidays slow replies; plan the schedule around them.
